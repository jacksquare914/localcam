## What this check establishes

- The three MIPI pairs land on the vendor's own CSI RX D1/CK0/D0 pairs, in that
  order, and every one of those pads is in the **1.8 V** IO domain.
- `CAM_SCL`/`CAM_SDA` are on **I2C4_M2** (pads 16/17), which Luckfox labels as the
  MIPI camera I2C. Both pads are 1.8 V, which is why the pull-ups go to `+1V8` and not
  `+3V3` — pulling them to 3.3 V would over-drive 1.8 V pins.
- `VBUS_DET` is on pad 24, `USB_VBUSDET`, in the **3.3 V** domain. That is the
  reason the divider had to move off `+3V3`: 100k/100k from 3.3 V gives 1.65 V, which is
  below a 3.3 V input's V_IH, so the pin sat in the indeterminate band. From `+5V` it is
  2.5 V, a valid logic high.
- `ADC0_RECOV` is on `SARADC_IN0` (pad 26), a **1.8 V** input — hence the 1 k series
  resistor between it and the exposed header pin.
- Pads 77/78 are `VCC_1V8`/`VCC_3V3` and pads 79/80/81 are `VCC5V0_SYS`. Only 5 V
  goes **in**; 1.8 V and 3.3 V come **out** of the module's own PMIC.
- Pad 76 is `VCC3V3_RTC` and is deliberately left unconnected. See the design intent.
- Pads the vendor marks as taken by fitted options are avoided: pad 37 (eMMC),
  pads 48, 63 and 68 (WiFi module).
